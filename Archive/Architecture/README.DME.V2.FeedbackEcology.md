## Unified Runtime Continuum

Runtime attentional geometry is the active continuum.
Projection, Retention, Agency, Proof, Governance, Decision Topology, and Ledger are derived coordinate regimes or control surfaces over that continuum.

They do not replace runtime.
They regulate how runtime-derived structure is exposed, retained, challenged, acted upon, recorded, or authorized.
