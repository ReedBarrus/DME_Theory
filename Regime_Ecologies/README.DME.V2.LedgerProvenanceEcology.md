## 1. Purpose

This document defines the Ledger / Provenance Ecology of DME V2.

Ledger / Provenance is a cross-cutting support regime.

It governs reconstructable causal history across:

```text
source admission
structural signatures
distinction formation
runtime activation
projection
attention envelopes
commitments
decision topology
execution receipts
consequence signatures
feedback records
integrated consequence records
retention posture
proof traces
authority deltas
reference candidates
archive migration
```

It answers:

```text
What can be reconstructed?
What lineage is preserved?
What changed?
Through what operator path?
Under what lens and loss?
What decisions were considered?
What branches were rejected, simulated, blocked, permitted, or executed?
What consequences returned?
What feedback updated future topology?
What can be queried later?
What may curve future runtime?
```

Ledger / Provenance is not merely storage.

It is the ecology of accountable causal continuity.

## 2. Core Thesis

```text
Ledger / Provenance Ecology is the regime of reconstructable causal history.
```

Compressed:

```text
Ledger = record substrate.
Provenance = structural dynamics over transformation history.
ProvenanceTopology = queryable causal geometry of lineage, decision, consequence, feedback, and authority.
LedgerFold = topology-preserving integration of records into reconstructable causal structure.
LedgerCurvature = historical pressure from provenance topology into future runtime.
```

Core law:

```text

```

## 3. Why Ledger / Provenance Exists

DME requires more than memory.

It requires accountable memory.

Without Ledger / Provenance, DME risks collapse such as:

```text
memory → truth
record → proof
receipt → authority
projection summary → source
execution success → legitimacy
feedback → final meaning
archive → deletion
retention echo → current support
```

Ledger / Provenance prevents these collapses by preserving lineage, transformation, receipts, reconstruction paths, and scoped causal relations.

It ensures future runtime can ask not only:

```text
What do we remember?
```

but:

```text
Where did this come from?
How did it transform?
What was lost?
What was decided?
What consequence followed?
What feedback returned?
What can be reconstructed?
What may this safely influence now?
```
