## Key Principles

1. Own your data. Your content, your metadata, your identity.
2. Use & publish visible data for humans first, machines second.
3. Make what you need.
4. Use what you make!
5. Document your stuff.
6. Open source your stuff!
7. UX and design is more important than protocols, formats, data models, schemas, or APIs.
8. Modularity. Building blocks, not all-in-one products. Interoperability, not complaints.
9. Longevity. Build for the long web. Lifetime ownership of your domain and your data.
10. Pluralism. This is a people-focused community that values different approaches to solving problems, as well as different ways of thinking, backgrounds, and skills.
11. Above all, Have fun.
